    Your private document workspace

    Choose Redact a PDF to create a permanently redacted file for sharing. Choose Prepare text for AI to protect pasted wording before copying it into your own AI tool, then bring its edited reply back to restore private details. Veil does not connect to AI. Settings contains sensitivity and phrase search; Work contains saved work and Clear session.

    Check private details

    On PDFs, Add and Remove brush over characters; Inspect opens precise text selection. Use Area for signatures, pictures, handwriting and other content without recognised text. On pasted and converted text, select wording to protect it, or click a highlighted section to remove protection. Check identifying context too: suggestions are not guaranteed anonymisation.

    Redact & download

    Creates a permanently redacted PDF. Inspect every page of the generated preview before downloading. Original PDF pages become masked images with no hidden source text, attachments or restoration key. Pasted and converted text becomes a new text PDF containing only the redacted wording.

    Edit privately with AI (Beta)

    1. Choose this after checking protection. Supported single-column PDF letters keep their letterhead and footer. Supported text PDFs can retain text styles and simple dividers. Unsupported layouts offer a simpler text layout before you continue. Word and email uploads are not available in this release. Word remains an output option for the revised wording.
    2. Add your objective, copy the complete prompt and paste into your chosen AI chat. Expand the prompt to check for remaining sensitive details.
    3. Review the readable draft. A or a approves; R or r asks for revisions. The final reply looks like code: copy it whole, without editing.
    4. Choose I have the AI’s reply, paste it, and preview. Veil checks the structure and protected placeholders before restoring private details on this device.
    5. Review the wording and choose Word, PDF, formatted copy or plain text. Longer text wraps and adds pages automatically.

    AI editing and copying protected text are available for pasted text. For PDFs, use Redact & download. PDF text sharing is unavailable because it can include hidden words.

    Headings and supported font/emphasis settings are retained where available. Unchanged passages keep their inline formatting. Rewritten passages keep supported source styles when the reply identifies them; otherwise they use clean body formatting. PDF uses an embedded sans-serif font. Word and pasted output depend on fonts and clipboard support in the destination. Supported PDF letterhead artwork stays on this device and is retained in document downloads. Word has an editable body with picture-based letterhead and footer. Unsupported layouts, body graphics, table structures and automatic numbering may require a text-only fallback. Link destinations are discarded. No manual box-fitting is required.

    Save and continue later

    Your work lives in this tab’s memory. Work → Save and continue later downloads a private .veil snapshot. Open that file in this version of Veil on another device to continue. It includes the unredacted working document, private details and validated edits: never upload it to AI. Password protection is optional. Without it, anyone with the file can read it. Save again after changes; text not yet successfully previewed in the AI reply box is not saved as an approved edit.

    Earlier saved work can be reopened; saved local wording is retained, while old text-box positions are replaced by automatic flow. Files saved by newer versions may need that same or a compatible newer Veil version.

    Back and Forward move between visited tool screens while this session exists. Leaving or refreshing the tool may trigger the browser’s standard warning; its wording and availability are controlled by the browser, and mobile browsers may omit it. Save before leaving. Clearing releases app references; it is not a forensic wipe.

    No OCR: scanned text, image text, forms and annotations need manual masking. PDF extraction can have reading-order errors. Check what you share and what you restore. Placeholder checks cannot establish whether an AI changed meaning. Unsupported encrypted/XFA documents and excessive input sizes are rejected.
